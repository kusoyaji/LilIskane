# MEDIA REQUESTS

Footage and stills the design needs and Chaabi does not yet have. Written to be
handed straight to an AI image-to-video pass or to a shoot brief.

**House rules for everything below**

- Delivered material is never colour-graded toward the renders, and renders are
  never graded toward the photographs. The whole argument of the site rests on
  the two being honestly comparable; matching them would quietly destroy it.
- Hard Marrakech sun, real black shadow. No haze, no teal-orange grade, no
  drone-cinematic bloom.
- Every clip must hold up as a still, because its first frame is the poster the
  majority of mobile users will ever see.
- Delivery: ProRes or 10-bit source; we transcode. AV1 + H.265 fallback, plus a
  WebP poster extracted from frame 1.
- Nothing autoplays with audio. Nothing is longer than 6 s.

---

## 1. Hero — the settle *(highest priority)*

**Why:** the home hero is currently a static render. A 4-second easing-to-rest
move would make the first screen feel inhabited without costing legibility.

- **Subject:** `rg2_Ext_Cam_c1_jardin_1` — the limestone facade with the
  moucharabieh screens, seen down the pedestrian allée.
- **Movement:** slow dolly forward along the allée, roughly 1.5 m of travel,
  decelerating to a complete stop. No orbit, no crane, no parallax pop.
- **Duration:** 4 s, ending on a full 1.5 s of stillness so the loop point is
  invisible.
- **Start frame:** exactly the existing render.
- **End frame:** same composition, ~8 % tighter, palm fronds having moved
  slightly in the breeze.
- **Must not:** introduce people, cars, or birds. The frame is architecture.

## 2. Proof pairs — matched camera moves *(highest priority)*

**Why:** the render/photograph comparison is the site's central device. Right
now the two frames are static and framed differently, which is honest but makes
the comparison harder than it needs to be.

For each of the seven pairs, we need **the photograph re-shot from the render's
camera position** — same focal length, same height, same angle. Not a video
request; a shoot request at Riad Garden I.

| Pair | Render | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Façade | `rg2_Ext_Cam_c1_jardin_1` | RG1 facade from the allée, same eye height |
| Piscine | `rg2_TypeB_3` | RG1 pool from the same corner, same time of day |
| Séjour | `rg2_Sejour_v2` | RG1 living room, camera at the same wall |
| Chambre | `rg2_Chambre_Parentale_` | RG1 bedroom, bed on the same axis |
| Chambre enfants | `rg2_Chambre_Enfants` | RG1 second bedroom |
| Cuisine | `rg2_Cuisine_v2` | RG1 kitchen, portrait to match |
| SDB | `rg2_SDB_V2` | RG1 bathroom, portrait to match |

Shoot at the same hour as the render's stated sun angle (late morning).
**Do not stage.** Delivered flats photographed as lived in are more persuasive
than restaged ones, and the existing RG1 kitchen — sage cabinets, a mint
fridge — is the single most convincing image in the entire library precisely
because it looks like somebody's.

## 3. Camera path — street to salon

**Why:** the project page sequence currently cross-fades three stills. Real
continuity needs the moves to connect.

Three clips, each 3 s, that end and begin on matching framings so they can be
played back to back:

1. **Approach.** From the commerces at street level, moving toward the
   residence entrance. Ends framed on the gate.
2. **Through.** From the gate into the planted courtyard. Ends looking across
   the pool.
3. **Inside.** From the terrace threshold into the living room. Ends on the
   seating area, matching `rg2_Sejour_v2`.

Constant height (~1.6 m, eye level — not drone height). Constant speed. The
cut points must match within a few degrees or the continuity reads as three
separate videos, which is exactly what we are trying to avoid.

## 4. Delivered-phase establishing clip

**Why:** the strongest asset is that phase I exists. We have stills; a moving
shot of the finished, occupied programme would be worth more than any render.

- **Subject:** Riad Garden I, the pool and surrounding blocks.
- **Movement:** slow lateral track, 5 s.
- **Content:** residents present and unstaged — this is the one place people
  belong in frame, because the point is that it is lived in.
- **Time:** late afternoon, long shadows.
